Entry is free and online. Entrants must be at least 13; ages 13–17 need parental or guardian permission. Teams may have up to four members, each person may join one team, and each team may submit one project. Build and submit during the published window; credit any prior work. AI tools are allowed with attribution and responsibility for their output. The entry needs an accessible demo or source, trying instructions, the required walkthrough, credits and at least one limitation.

The advertised pool is **USD 100 in Featherless API credits plus certificates — non-cash**, not a cash prize.

Judging weights: usefulness 35%, execution 30%, clarity 20%, originality 15%.

**Preparation status:** complete source package; not submitted. Publishing the source or saving a draft is not the final competition submission. Personal onboarding and eligibility declarations must be completed accurately by the entrant.
